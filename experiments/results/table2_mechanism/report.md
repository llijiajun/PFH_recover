## Reading of the result

W1 renormalizes the spectrum over j <= 512, so it measures the shape of the
frequency distribution and ignores the number of types. The raw thinned
spectrum is a sparse copy of the truth and keeps a similar normalized shape
for Zipf populations, but it loses most types. The fitted inverse restores
the missing types with a prior, which shifts mass into the singleton bin and
moves the normalized shape away from the truth at low rates. The table
reports both quantities so the trade-off is visible.
