The 25-standard-deviation cutoff discards less than 1e-11 posterior mass on every configuration in the grid. Raising the constant to 50 lowers that bound by ten further orders of magnitude, while lowering it to 10 or below leaves a non-negligible tail on some configurations. The constant is a conservative numerical support cutoff fixed before evaluation and is not tuned against ground truth. No optimality claim is made for the constant and no recovery experiment is rerun here.
